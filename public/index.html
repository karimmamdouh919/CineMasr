OUR STORY

Cinema as it was meant to be.

Since 1947, CineMisr has been the city's premier destination for film. We've screened world premieres, hosted legendary directors, and introduced generations to the transformative power of cinema.

Today, we operate twelve screens across three buildings — all equipped with 4K laser projection and Dolby Atmos sound, without sacrificing the architectural warmth of our historic spaces.
